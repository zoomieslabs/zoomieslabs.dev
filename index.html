Small, sharp tools that respect your attention.

ZoomiesLabs is a small, independent studio. We make open-source developer tools that run on your machine, do one job well, and stay quiet until you ask for them.

The name comes from the zoomies, the sudden bursts of energy a dog gets when it races around the house. Anyone with ADHD knows the pattern: a burst of focus, real momentum, and then the thread slips. We build for the moment you come back.

retramo

Pick up where you left off.

Our first tool is a VS Code extension for the moment you sit back down. It tells you what you were in the middle of, what changed while you were away, and what to review first.

It doesn't care who changed the code: Copilot, Claude Code, a teammate or a git pull. Free, MIT-licensed, and nothing leaves your machine unless you turn it on.
