retramo

by ZoomiesLabs

Pick up where you left off.

When you come back after an interruption, Retramo tells you what you were in the middle of, what changed while you were away, and what to review first. Made for people with ADHD, and for anyone who works alongside AI agents.

01 · Install

Free, and ready in a minute.

VS Code Marketplace Open VSX (Cursor, VSCodium, Windsurf)

ext install zoomieslabs.retramo

02 · How it works

Quiet until you need it.

  • Works with any agent

    Copilot, Claude Code, Cursor's agent, a teammate or a git pull. Retramo doesn't care who changed the code.

  • Everything stays on your machine

    No telemetry unless you opt in, and no code ever saved or sent by default.

  • Two actions, no setup

    “I'm leaving” and “I'm back”. It also saves a session on its own when you step away.
